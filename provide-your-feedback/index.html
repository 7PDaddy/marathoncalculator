Let's Make This Great

October 4, 2024 · What's Up

At MarathonCalculator.com, we're committed to providing the best possible tools for runners to set and achieve their marathon goals. But we know that no tool is perfect from the start, and that's where we need your help.

Why Your Feedback Matters

This website has always been about more than just offering a service, it's about building a community of runners who are inspired to push their limits. When MarathonCalculator.com was first launched over 16 years ago, the goal was simple: to help runners stay motivated by providing them with practical, easy-to-use tools for planning their races. Over the years, we've realized that the best way to keep improving is to listen to you, the people who actually use these tools.

Now, as we work on improving the site and adding new features, we want to hear from you. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or planning your first race, your input is invaluable.

  • What do you like?
  • What's missing?
  • How can we make this better?

Shaping the Future Together: Provide Your Feedback

We're considering making parts of the project publicly available, possibly open-source or at least available for others to learn from, contribute to, and use. While there might be small additions like ads to help fund this effort, our primary goal isn't profit, it's creating a platform that helps. We believe in reinvesting whatever success this site has back into making it a better resource for the running community.

We want MarathonCalculator.com to be a place where you feel like your goals are supported, and where you can contribute your thoughts to help us create tools that truly serve runners everywhere. Whether it's new calculators, training insights, or suggestions on making the site more user-friendly, we welcome it all.

Share Your Thoughts

This is where you come in. If you have ideas, suggestions, or just want to share your experience using MarathonCalculator.com, we'd love to hear from you. Your comments will help guide future updates and improvements. Ultimately, this site is built for you, so it's your input that will help shape its future.

The project is now open source: share ideas or report issues on GitHub, and together we can create a resource that will help runners at every stage of their journey.

Thank You!

Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback. We're excited to hear your thoughts and to continue building MarathonCalculator.com into something great, with your help.
