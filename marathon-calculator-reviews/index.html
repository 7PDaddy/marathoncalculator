Marathon Calculator Reviews

December 20, 2024 · Reviews

When it comes to planning your marathon, having the right tools can make all the difference. While we're excited about what MarathonCalculator.com is shaping up to be, we also want to celebrate some of the amazing calculators out there that runners have relied on for years and our brief marathon calculator reviews. Here's a roundup of some great options to check out:

1. McMillan Running Calculator

  • Website: McMillanRunning.com
  • Overview: A favorite among runners for years, this tool helps predict race times and recommends training paces based on your current performance. It's comprehensive and perfect for those who want tailored insights into training regimens.
  • What We Like: The depth of information it provides, including pacing for intervals, tempo runs, and long runs.
  • Drawbacks: It's more suited to experienced runners who understand the nuances of training.

2. Runner's World Race Time Predictor

  • Website: RunnersWorld.com
  • Overview: A simple yet effective calculator that uses recent race performances to predict your marathon time. Great for those who prefer a quick and straightforward tool.
  • What We Like: Easy to use and provides instant results.
  • Drawbacks: Limited customization options for training or pacing adjustments.

3. Jack Daniels' VDOT Running Calculator

  • Website: VDOTCalculator.com
  • Overview: Created by legendary coach Jack Daniels, this calculator is based on the VDOT system and helps determine optimal training paces and race predictions.
  • What We Like: Backed by science and highly respected in the running community.
  • Drawbacks: May feel overly technical for beginners.

4. MarathonGuide.com Pace Calculator

  • Website: MarathonGuide.com
  • Overview: A no-frills pace calculator that lets you input your target time to determine splits for every mile or kilometer.
  • What We Like: Straightforward and perfect for race day planning.
  • Drawbacks: Minimal features compared to other calculators.

5. FindMyMarathon Pace Calculator

  • Website: FindMyMarathon.com
  • Overview: This tool not only helps with pace calculations but also offers course-specific adjustments based on terrain, elevation, and weather.
  • What We Like: Great for runners tackling challenging courses.
  • Drawbacks: Slightly overwhelming interface for first-time users.
