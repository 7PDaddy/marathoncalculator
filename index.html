Built by a runner, not a media company

This site has one purpose: help you run your race. No ads, no signup, no tracking, no upsell. It's built marathon-first because 26.2 is the distance that changes people, but every tool works for the half, 10K, 5K, and custom distances too.

Behind it are 40+ years of running and 20+ marathon finishes, including plenty of hard lessons about fast starts and skipped long runs. The whole project is open source on GitHub: read the math, suggest a feature, or argue with our pacing philosophy. Here's how we built it.
