About Marathon Calculator

The story behind the site

At Marathon Calculator, we believe running is about much more than miles and finish times. It's about believing in yourself, staying consistent when things get tough, and discovering what you're capable of along the way. Whether you're training for your first race or chasing a new personal best, every step forward matters.

This site was created by a lifelong runner with more than 40 years of passion for the sport and over 20 marathon finishes. Marathon Calculator started with a simple idea: create free, easy-to-use tools that genuinely help runners stay motivated, set meaningful goals, and keep progressing.

Building this site has been a personal goal for nearly 20 years. Over the years, I launched different versions of calculators and running tools, always trying to create something more useful, motivating, and meaningful for runners. In 2024, I launched my favorite version so far, but this is still just the beginning.

Running has a way of teaching discipline, patience, confidence, and perseverance. Some days feel effortless, while others test your limits. That's what makes crossing the finish line so rewarding.

AI is also changing the running world quickly, and in 2026 we plan to introduce more tools designed to help runners set smarter goals, stay motivated, and better understand their training. But at the end of the day, achieving those goals is still up to you. The work, commitment, and determination will always belong to the runner.

More than anything, Marathon Calculator exists to encourage runners of every level to keep going, trust the process, and believe that big goals are possible one mile at a time.
